# Hawaiʻi Annual Report Requirements

Every corporation, LLC, limited partnership, and limited liability partnership registered to do business in Hawaiʻi must file an Annual Report with the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA), Business Registration Division (BREG). This filing keeps the state's public registry current and keeps your business in good standing. Below is what you need to know to stay compliant.

## Who Must File

Nearly all registered business entities must file an Annual Report, including:

- Domestic and foreign profit corporations
- Domestic and foreign nonprofit corporations
- Domestic and foreign limited liability companies (LLCs)
- Domestic and foreign limited partnerships (LPs)
- Limited liability partnerships (LLPs) and limited liability limited partnerships (LLLPs)

Sole proprietorships and general partnerships are not required to file an Annual Report.

New entities are exempt in their first calendar year of registration — the first Annual Report is due the year *following* formation.

## When It's Due

Hawaiʻi does not use a single statewide deadline. Instead, your due date is tied to the calendar quarter in which your business originally registered:

| Registration Date Falls Between | Annual Report Due By |
|---|---|
| January 1 – March 31 | March 31 |
| April 1 – June 30 | June 30 |
| July 1 – September 30 | September 30 |
| October 1 – December 31 | December 31 |

The report can be filed at any point during the applicable quarter — it does not have to wait until the deadline itself.

## What the Report Requires

The Annual Report is a status update, not a financial disclosure — it does not require revenue, income, or financial statements. It generally confirms:

- Current principal office and mailing address
- Registered agent name and address
- Names and addresses of officers/directors (corporations) or members/managers (LLCs)
- Whether an LLC is member-managed or manager-managed

## How to File

**Online (recommended):** File through the Hawaiʻi Business Express (HBE) portal. Online filing is processed fastest and allows you to sign up for free email reminders for future filing years.

**By mail:** Complete the appropriate paper form and mail it, with payment, to:

Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Business Registration Division, P.O. Box 40, Honolulu, HI 96810

**By email or fax:** Scanned or faxed forms are accepted, but credit card payment information must accompany the filing. Processing by email, mail, or fax typically takes longer than filing online.

**In person:** Visit the BREG office at 335 Merchant Street, Room 201, Honolulu, HI 96813.

## Filing Fees

Filing fees vary by entity type and filing method (online filings are generally slightly cheaper than paper filings):

| Entity Type | Typical Online Filing Fee |
|---|---|
| LLCs and Profit Corporations | $12.50 |
| Nonprofit Corporations | $2.50 |
| Partnerships | $5.00 |

Fees are subject to change — confirm the current fee schedule on the DCCA/BREG site or within the HBE portal before filing.

## What Happens If You Miss the Deadline

Failing to file on time puts your business out of good standing and can eventually lead to administrative dissolution (for domestic entities) or revocation of authority to transact business in Hawaiʻi (for foreign entities) after continued delinquency. Losing good standing can also disrupt banking, financing, and contracting relationships. If your report already on file contains an error, an amended Annual Report can be filed at no charge.

## Staying on Top of Future Filings

Because Hawaiʻi's due dates are quarter-based rather than tied to a fixed calendar date, it's easy to lose track of them. To avoid missing a deadline:

- File online so you're eligible for free automatic email reminders the following year
- Subscribe to "MyBusiness Notifications" on Hawaiʻi Business Express for a small annual fee
- Keep your registered agent and contact information current so state notices reach you
- Watch the BREG "What's New" page at the start of each filing quarter for reminder announcements

## Where to Get Help

For questions about a specific filing, contact the Business Registration Division at (808) 586-2727 (Monday–Friday, 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m. HST), or visit the official DCCA/BREG website. Be cautious of third-party companies soliciting payment for Annual Report filings — BREG does not authorize or use outside vendors to collect these filings on its behalf.

*Legal basis: Hawaiʻi's annual reporting requirement for LLCs is set out in HRS §428-210, with corresponding provisions governing corporations and partnerships under Title 23A of the Hawaiʻi Revised Statutes. This article is for general informational purposes and is not a substitute for reviewing current DCCA/BREG guidance or consulting an attorney for entity-specific questions.*
