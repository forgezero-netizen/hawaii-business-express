Startup roadmap

Start a Business in Hawaiʻi

Use this guide to orient yourself, then complete official registration and filing steps through the Hawaiʻi Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA) and other appropriate government agencies.

A practical sequence

  1. Research the marketClarify the customer, problem, competitors, pricing, and whether the business can support your goals.
  2. Create a basic business planDefine what you will sell, who you will serve, how you will reach customers, and how the business will make money.
  3. Choose a legal structureConsider ownership, liability, management, taxes, and administrative requirements before choosing an entity type.
  4. Search the business nameCheck the DCCA registry before investing heavily in branding or filing documents.
  5. Register with DCCA when requiredUse the official state portal for entity formation, registrations, and related filings.
  6. Address tax, licensing, and operating requirementsDepending on the business, you may need tax registrations, professional or vocational licenses, local permits, insurance, banking, or other compliance steps.
Important: Entity choice can affect liability and taxes. This site provides general information, not legal, accounting, or tax advice. Consider consulting a qualified attorney, certified public accountant, or tax professional.

Before you file

Questions worth answering first

Getting these decisions clear early can reduce rework after the business is registered.

Who owns the business?

Document expected ownership, management authority, and how major decisions will be made.

What structure fits?

Compare common structures such as sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, and limited liability companies.

Does the activity require licensing?

Registration of a business entity does not automatically satisfy professional, vocational, tax, or local operating requirements.

Name check

Search the registry before you commit to a name

A registry search can help you research existing entities. Final name acceptability is determined through the official filing process.
