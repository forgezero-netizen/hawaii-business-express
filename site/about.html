Independent by design

About Hawaiʻi Business Express

A private, independent information service designed to make Hawaiʻi business research and common business tasks easier to understand and navigate.

What this site does

Hawaiʻi Business Express organizes practical business information around the questions people commonly ask: How do I research a business? How do I start one? Where do I file? How do I maintain an existing registration?

For official business records, filings, certificates, and state services, this site sends users to the appropriate State of Hawaiʻi source rather than pretending to replace the government system.

What this site is not

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the State of Hawaiʻi, the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA), the Business Registration Division (BREG), or Hawaiʻi.gov.

It does not issue state registrations, make official determinations, or maintain the authoritative Hawaiʻi business registry.

How search works

When you enter a business name into a search form on this site, the browser opens the official DCCA business-search page in a new tab with your search term. The official DCCA site displays and controls the search results.

Guiding principle

Make the official source easier to reach—not harder to identify.

We clearly identify when a link or search sends you to a government site, and we keep the independent-service disclosure visible throughout the site.

Future business-service recommendations

Future versions may include service-provider recommendations or referrals. If compensation is received for a referral or affiliate relationship, that relationship will be disclosed where the recommendation appears.
