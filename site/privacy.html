Site policy

Privacy

How this website handles basic usage information and external links.

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Information this site uses

Hawaiʻi Business Express currently does not require user accounts, accept payments, or operate a web contact form. If you choose to email us, the information you include in that email is received through the email service used by you and by Hawaiʻi Business Express.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and how visitors interact with pages and features. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as device, browser, approximate location, referral source, and interaction data according to Google’s own policies and settings.

The site records an analytics event when the business-search feature is submitted, but it is configured not to intentionally send the business name entered in the search field as part of that custom event.

Business searches

The business name entered into a search box is used in your browser to construct a link to the official Hawaiʻi Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA) search page. The DCCA page opens in a new tab. Once you visit a government or other external site, that site’s privacy practices apply.

External links

This website links to DCCA and other external resources. Hawaiʻi Business Express does not control the privacy practices, security, or content of those external sites.

Future changes

If this website later adds accounts, subscriptions, paid services, contact forms, monitoring, advertising, or affiliate services, this privacy notice will be updated to describe the additional information practices before or when those features are introduced.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@hawaiibusinessexpress.com.
