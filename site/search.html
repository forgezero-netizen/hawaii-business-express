Official-record handoff

Search Hawaiʻi Businesses

Enter a business name here. Your search will open the official Hawaiʻi Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA) business search results in a new tab.

The results page is hosted by the State of Hawaiʻi DCCA. This site does not copy, store, or modify the state registry.

Search tip: If you do not find the business on the first attempt, try a shorter version of the name or remove punctuation and entity suffixes such as LLC or Inc.
How this works

Authoritative data stays with DCCA

Hawaiʻi Business Express does not maintain a duplicate business-registration database. The search field sends your query to the official DCCA search page so the state remains the source of record.

Open the official DCCA search directly

After you search

What you can use the state record for

DCCA business records can help you confirm that an entity is registered and review public information associated with that record. For official filings, certificates, or purchased documents, continue through DCCA.

Confirm registration

Use the state record to verify that a business appears in the Hawaiʻi registry and review the entity record.

Review public details

Available information may include entity history and people or agents listed in public filings.

Purchase official documents

When available, official copies and certificates should be obtained directly through the DCCA portal.
